## Part 1: Why

One day, I read an article about how religion and social background influence people's perceptions of color. I wondered what color came to mind when my friends thought of me.

So I asked 30 friends I'd spent time with over the previous two years to each choose a color. I plotted their choices in the [OKLab color space](https://bottosson.github.io/posts/oklab/) to look at their distances and distribution (Fig. 1).

## Part 2: Analysis

I grouped the colors in OKLab using [DBSCAN](https://scikit-learn.org/stable/modules/generated/sklearn.cluster.DBSCAN.html), choosing eps = 0.12 and min_samples = 3 after testing several settings.

This produced four clusters and five unassigned colors. I averaged the OKLab coordinates of the 13 colors in the largest cluster and converted the result to sRGB: #1D2D8E.

Somehow, this blue feels like a reflection of how my friends see me. ChatGPT described it as a deep, highly saturated blue with a hint of violet, calling it Deep Royal Blue and associating it with rationality, calm, and order. I wasn’t going to argue with that, so I decided to use it as this website's accent color.
